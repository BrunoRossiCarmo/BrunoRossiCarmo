### 🙌 Hello There! 🙌

I'm a **Physics** student at the University of São Paulo - IFSC. 
Previously, I was studying Biomedical Informatics but decided to switch to physics. It might seem like "Biomedical Informatics" and physics are worlds apart, but they intersect more than you might think. If you're curious about the Biomedical Informatics course, check out its [page](https://ibm.fmrp.usp.br/), [Institutional Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpGVfwBcSn0), and also the [Institutional Video from Physics-IFSC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6kf1sB_i8S0&ab_channel=PRG.ONLINEUSP).
